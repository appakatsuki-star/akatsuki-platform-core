# Final Founder and Reviewer Signoff Template

## Instructions

This form is blank and unapproved. Check exactly the intended option, name the scope, attach evidence, and obtain every reviewer required by that scope. Unchecked or conflicting options mean `NO-GO`.

## Founder decision

- [ ] I approve a Phase 1 internal MVP build, limited to the scope written below and subject to all required reviewer conditions.
- [ ] I do not approve Phase 1 yet.
- [ ] I approve only Sprint 1 foundation work or the named ticket(s), not the full Phase 1.
- [ ] I require no real customer funds, payment capture/refund/settlement, or financial promises.
- [ ] I require no public production launch or public customer access.
- [ ] I accept the proposed MVP scope as a direction: one tenant/provider, USD, Games/PUBG-like product/packages, reviewed publication, with exclusions recorded.
- [ ] I request changes before any Phase 1 work.
