# MVP Decision Summary

## How to use this document

This document turns every Phase 0.5 blocker into a proposal. “Founder approval” means approval of business direction, cost, risk, or scope. It does not replace qualified legal, security, accounting, or infrastructure review where named.

Recommended approval status values are: `Approved`, `Needs change`, or `Not decided`.

## Founder decision bundle

The founder should directly approve DEC-001–009, the commercial parts of DEC-011–013, provider/region/budget choices in DEC-014 and DEC-018–021, and DEC-029. Technical owners must approve DEC-010 and DEC-014–022 implementation controls. Founder approval cannot certify accounting correctness, legal authority, security evidence, or provider capability.

## Catalog publishing decisions included in DEC-003, DEC-004, DEC-007, and DEC-008

The following are part of the recommended option and require founder approval with the MVP scope:

- A **Provider Product** is a raw synchronized provider record. It is never customer-visible by import alone.
- A **Store Category** and **Store Product** are tenant-owned customer-facing presentation records.
- A **Product Package / Variant** is the sellable option and may map to one provider service.
- Publishing mode is explicit: `ADD_AS_PACKAGE` or `ADD_AS_STANDALONE_PRODUCT`.
- Game top-ups normally publish one Store Product with provider services as packages. Quantity-based SMM-like services normally publish as standalone products, but SMM remains outside the recommended MVP.
- Dynamic input definitions are reviewed and mapped before publication; provider metadata cannot silently change a published customer form.
- Tenant visuals override provider visuals. Provider images are fallback candidates only and never bypass review.
- Customer prices derive from snapshotted provider cost plus approved tier markup; agent commission and net platform/admin profit are calculated and snapshotted separately.
- Sync, mapping, publish/unpublish, visual, pricing/tier, credential, and provider-order actions are permissioned and audited.

## Proposed overall position

Approve the direction as a package, then obtain the named specialist evidence. Until both founder and specialist approvals are recorded, all rows remain proposals and Phase 1 remains `NO-GO`.
